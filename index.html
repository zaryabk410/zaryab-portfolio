Questions clients ask

    

      

        Which Odoo versions do you work with?
        
Odoo 14 through 19, Community and Enterprise. I also migrate custom modules between versions, for example 14 to 17.

      

      

        Can my staff use Odoo from their phones without buying more licenses?
        
Yes. I build Flutter apps with their own login that talk to Odoo over a secure API, so employees can check in, request leave or register visitors without an Odoo user seat each.

      

      

        Do you connect Odoo to other systems?
        
Regularly. I've built REST integrations with Oracle Fusion, JSON-RPC bridges for mobile apps, and scripts that migrate Excel data into Odoo.

      

      

        Do you take freelance or remote projects?
        
Yes, alongside my full-time role. I'm in Riyadh (GMT+3) and work with teams in Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and beyond.

      

      

        What happens after the module goes live?
        
I stay on for fixes, training and small improvements. Most of my clients come back with a second module once the first one is in use.
